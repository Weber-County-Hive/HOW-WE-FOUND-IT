Tactical Campaigns, the PAC and the party: how we traced the money.

A campaign firm, a political action committee and the Utah Republican Party all show up in the same 2026 money reports. This page shows each step we took to trace them, and the public record behind each step.

The short version

  • It started with a tip about Joe DeBose and his firm, Tactical Campaigns. We checked every part of it against public records.
  • Candidates and PACs paid Tactical $131,351.81 in 2026. The Utah Republican Party reported 14 entries from Tactical totaling $71,600, listed as contributions with no purpose given.
  • A PAC called Prosperous Utah Communities took in $70,000 and paid $50,000 to Right Hook Strategies, a company whose only member is Tactical's Michael Melendez. It also paid Tactical $4,904.99.
  • The PAC reported paying the party $8,730 in three payments. The party's report lists the same three amounts as coming from Brent Bowles' campaign. Bowles' own report lists one payment to the party, $11,695.
  • The PAC's treasurer, Mike McCauley, lists his office at 420 E South Temple, Suite 390. That's the address on the party's federal filings and the address the party requires on postage-paid mailers.
  • The records show who paid whom and when. They don't say why. A matching amount or address is a match only.
What was disclosed. Every amount on this page is public because it was reported. The PAC is registered and filed its 2026 reports. The party, Tactical's paying campaigns and Rep. Alvey filed theirs with the state. Brent Bowles filed his pre-primary report with Utah County, dated June 15, before the June 23 primary.

How this was checked

  • Utah Republican Party (State), 2026: the full report downloaded from disclosures.utah.gov on Nov. 18, 2026. All 491 lines read, then totaled by script.
  • Prosperous Utah Communities, 2026: the full report downloaded from disclosures.utah.gov on Nov. 18, 2026. All 14 lines read, then totaled by script.
  • Payments to Tactical Campaigns: disclosures.utah.gov Advanced Search, Expenditures, 2026, payee "Tactical Campaigns," Nov. 18, 2026. 20 result lines copied and added up.
  • Business records: Utah Division of Corporations records for Tactical, Right Hook Strategies, Veracity Enterprises, Hemmco and the Prosperous Utah Communities nonprofit, read on screen or from copied records (June 27 and Nov. 18, 2026).
  • Brent Bowles: his handwritten "7 Days before Primary" report, dated June 15, 2026, from Utah County's financial declarations list. Read by eye, page by page.
  • Federal filings: the Utah Republican Party's FEC Form 1 (July 20, 2024, and Oct. 30, 2025) and a May 2025 Form 3X. Text pulled from the filings.
  • Party rules: the Utah GOP County Leadership Handbook's "Indicia Mailing" section, read on the live page Nov. 18, 2026.
  • Not included: anything filed after Nov. 18, 2026, and Tactical's 2024 and 2025 records.

How we found it

  1. A tipIt started with a tip about Joe DeBose and Tactical Campaigns. We treated it as a lead only.Record: none yet. A tip is where we start, not what we report.
  2. Our research databaseWe searched our own database of earlier research. It had June 2026 business records for Tactical and Right Hook Strategies, and Utah lobbyist register records.Records: Utah Division of Corporations; Utah lobbyist register
  3. State campaign money reportsWe searched every 2026 payment to Tactical, then downloaded the Utah Republican Party's full 2026 report and read all 491 lines.Record: disclosures.utah.gov
  4. State business recordsThe Division of Corporations shows who owns each company: Tactical, Right Hook Strategies, Veracity Enterprises, Hemmco, and a nonprofit with the PAC's name.Record: Utah Division of Corporations business search
  5. Following the PACThe PAC's registration names its officers. Its 2026 report shows $70,000 in and where it went out.Records: PAC Statement of Organization; PAC 2026 report
  6. County reportsBrent Bowles ran for Utah County Commission, so he files with the county, not the state. We found his handwritten report on the county's list.Record: Utah County financial declarations
  7. Party rules and federal filingsThe party handbook explains the postage program and the address it requires on mailers. The party's FEC filings show the same address.Records: Utah GOP County Leadership Handbook; FEC Form 1
  8. Lining them upWe set the reports side by side. Three amounts show up on the PAC's report and the party's report under different payer names. Jim Alvey's postage amounts match the party's report exactly.Records: all of the above, compared line by line

Offices named in the filings, not drawn to scale. Home addresses are left off.

Who they are

  • Tactical Campaigns LLCA campaign firm in Provo, formed May 30, 2017. Its members are Joe DeBose, listed in Marion, Illinois, and Michael Melendez. Campaign reports describe its work as strategy and marketing, video, data, mailers, TV ads and text messages.
  • Joe (William Joseph) DeBoseA member of Tactical Campaigns. Its business record lists him in Marion, Illinois.
  • Michael MelendezA member of Tactical and the only member of Right Hook Strategies. He is a registered lobbyist for the Libertas Institute, a Utah think tank, and Ballotpedia lists him as its executive vice president (Sept. 2025).
  • Right Hook Strategies LLCFormed Oct. 30, 2023. Besides the PAC's $50,000, it was paid $10,000 by Preserve Orem (Oct. 28, 2025) and $20,000 by Daniel McCay's campaign (June 8, 2026).
  • Prosperous Utah CommunitiesA registered political action committee since Nov. 1, 2019. A nonprofit with the same name and address was formed two days earlier and dissolved in 2021.
  • David Herring and Veracity Enterprises LLCHerring is a PAC director and a registered lobbyist since 2019. He manages Veracity Enterprises, which describes itself as a political consulting firm. The state lists Veracity as administratively dissolved on April 21, 2026, before the PAC's June 2026 payments to it. Veracity gave the PAC $19,591 in 2024.
  • Walter PlumbA PAC director. The PAC's record lists him as self-employed at 201 S Main St, Suite 2000, Salt Lake City, the same office address listed for Salt Lake attorney Walter J. Plumb III.
  • Hemmco LLC (Dan Hemmert)Dan Hemmert's lobbying firm. Hemmert was a state senator (SD-14, 2016 to Jan. 2021), then Gov. Spencer Cox's first executive director of the Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity (2021 to Dec. 2022). He lobbied as Hemmco from 2023 until August 2026, when he became executive director of the Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA). Hemmco's $50,000 to the PAC came in June 2026.
  • John PestanaThe PAC lists John Pestana of Provo. Omniture co-founder and ObservePoint CEO John Pestana lives in Provo and chairs the Libertas Institute's board, where Michael Melendez works. That is a name-and-city match; the PAC's report lists no job for him.
  • Mike McCauley, CPA (McCauley & Associates)A Salt Lake political compliance firm. McCauley is the PAC's treasurer. His office, 420 E South Temple, Suite 390, is also the address the party uses on its federal filings, and the address the House Speaker Leadership PAC uses on Rep. Mike Ivory's reports.
  • Utah Republican Party (State)Holds the nonprofit postage permit (#1863) used for candidate mailers. Chair Robert Axson; treasurer Jason Knavel.
  • Brent BowlesA Republican candidate for Utah County Commission, Seat A. He was out in the June primary.
  • Rep. Jim AlveyUtah House District 10, Weber County.

What isn't known

What Tactical's $71,600 was for

The party lists Tactical's 14 entries as contributions with no purpose, and they aren't marked as in-kind. Whether any of it paid for candidates' postage isn't shown.

Why one set of payments has two payer names

The PAC says it paid the party $8,730. The party lists the same amounts under Bowles' campaign. The records don't say which is right, or whether both are.

$11,695 or $11,680

Bowles' report shows $11,695, handwritten. The party shows $11,680 on the same day, twice. Whether this is one payment, two, or a reading of the handwriting isn't known.

Bowles' later report

On the county's list, the "30 Days after Primary" spot for Bowles is blank. We didn't ask the county whether a report was filed.

"David Herring" and "David Kyle Kerring"

The PAC lists director David Herring of Orem. The same-name nonprofit lists David Kyle Kerring of Orem. Veracity's manager is listed as David Kyle Herring. The spellings are as filed. The records don't say whether they are one person.

Thomas Young

The tip named Thomas Young as part of Tactical's polling work. The 2026 records don't name him. A 2023 national polling conference program lists a course he taught on using cell phone tracking for predictive polling.

Questions to ask, and who to ask

  • Utah Republican Party: What were Tactical Campaigns' 14 entries ($71,600) for? Why are three payments the PAC says it made listed under Brent Bowles' campaign?
  • Utah County Elections: Did Brent Bowles file a report for 30 days after the primary?
  • Lieutenant Governor's elections office: When a candidate, PAC or vendor pays for mail postage through the party, how should each side report it?
